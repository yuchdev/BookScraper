## Verdict

No CRITICAL or HIGH findings. All new packages (`mongomock`, `pytz`, `sentinels`) are
dev-only dependencies — they appear in `[dependency-groups] dev` and are not included in
the published wheel, so there is no copyleft or license-compatibility risk to the
distribution model. The two outdated pins (`anthropic`, `pymongo`) are minor/patch bumps
with no associated security advisories; bumping them is optional routine maintenance, not
a blocker.

Do not block merge on this audit.
